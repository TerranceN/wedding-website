Poland is is a great starting point for visiting much of **Central** and **Eastern Europe**, including the **Balkans** (e.g. Croatia and Slovenia) as well as **Nordic Countries**. There are not a lot of direct flights to Poland, so consider looking for extended layovers in **London**, **Paris**, **Amsterdam** or **Berlin**.
