###### What are the Tatras?
The Tatras are a high mountain range located in the Western Carpathians that straddle the border between southern Poland and Northern Slovakia. Tatra National Park is accessible from the Polish or Slovakian side but this guide is focused on the Polish side.

###### What is Hut-to-Hut hiking?
Poland has a strong hiking culture and there are over 100 remote huts (“schroniska”) scattered throughout the mountains and countryside built specifically for hikers to eat and spend the night. It’s like backpacking but without the gear, and with warm treats and cool beer at every stop!
<br/>
<br/>
There are seven mountain huts located on the Polish side of Tatra National Park, but only a few are located in the alpine region known as the High Tatras (“Wysokie Tatry”) which is where are recommendations are focused. These huts are very popular and we recommend booking well in advance.

###### Planning your Route
In this section we will cover our recommended route, as well as alternative options and tips. The [official hiking map](https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/#49.21526/20.07290/15) will be your friend, but below is a high level overview of the options.
<br/>
<br/>
**Zakopane**
<br/>
Most routes start in Zakopane, either directly or from a trailhead a short bus-ride away.  If you are taking our recommended route, plan to arrive in Zakopane before noon. If your route has a longer first leg, you may need to book a night in Zakopane beforehand. 
<br/>
<br/>
We recommend booking at least one night in Zakopane after your hike and treating yourself to some of that mountain spa culture. We loved our stay at Hotel Sabała!
<br/>
<br/>
If you have booked a hotel for before or after your hike, the front desk should be able to store any unecessary luggage for you during your hike. Please call ahead of time to confirm.
<br/>
<br/>
**The High Tatras**
<br/>
Our recommendations are centered around the most popular routes and huts seen on the center right of the map below. Look for Kuźnice, Hala Gąsienicowa, Dolina Pięciu Stawów, and Morskie Oko.

##### Points of Interest

###### **Kuźnice** (pronounced _“Koo-jni-tseh”_)

![Kuźnice](./Images/HikingGuideKuznice.webp#float-left)

The starting point for our recommended hikes and where you pay your entrance fee for accessing Tatra National Park. Accessible by bus, taxi or hike from Zakopane. Has bathrooms, food and convenience items available for purchase before your trip. If you are not going hut-to-hut hiking you can still access Kasprowy Wierch (“Ka-spro-vih Vier-h”), one of the Tatra’s tallest peaks, by cable car from here.

<div class="clear"></div>

###### **Hala Gąsienicowa** (pronounced _“Hala Go-Sheh-Nee-Tso-Vah”_, translates to “Caterpillar Hall”)

![Hala Gąsienicowa](./Images/HikingGuideHalaGasienicowa.webp#float-left)

Your first introduction to the high Tatras. Hala Gąsienicowa is a ~3 hour one-way hike from Kuźnice and is often completed as a day trip. On route you will have views of the rolling Polish countryside and at the top you will be rewarded with a mountain skyline. Hala Gąsienicowa is a traditional sheep pasture and you will pass by a number of shepherds huts along the route. Pink flowers bloom in August and will likely no longer be visible in September.

<div class="clear"></div>

###### **Murowaniec** (pronounced _“Moo-ro-vah-niets”_)

![Murowaniec](./Images/HikingGuideMurowaniec.webp#float-left)

This hut turned 100 last year! Murowaniec is located beside Hala Gąsienicowa and is one of the most popular huts in the Tatras. We stayed here on our first night of hiking.

<div class="clear"></div>

###### **Dolina Pięciu Stawów**  (pronounced _“Doh-Lee-Nah Pien-Choo Sta-Voov”_, translates to “Valley of Five Ponds”)

![Dolina Pięciu Stawów](./Images/HikingGuideDolinaPieciuStawow1.webp#float-left)
![Dolina Pięciu Stawów](./Images/HikingGuideDolinaPieciuStawow2.webp#float-left)

This is where we got engaged! Dolina Pięciu Stawów is one of the most beautiful and secluded places in the Tatras. It includes a series of Alpine lakes as well as a waterfall. If you do not have time to cross the valley itself, we recommend at least hiking to a view point like Zawrat, Krzyżne, Kępa or Szpiglasowy Wierch.

<div class="clear"></div>

###### **Morskie Oko** (pronounced _“More-Skieh Oh-Koh”_, translates to “Eye of the Sea”)

![Morskie Oko](./Images/HikingGuideMorskieOko.webp#float-left)

The largest lake in the Tatra mountains, Morskie Oko is easily accessible as a day trip from Zakopane and is one of the most touristy destinations. Be prepared for crowds! There is a hut at Morskie Oko but we have not stayed here.

<div class="clear"></div>

<br/>
**Our Recommended Route:**
- Bus/taxi to Kuźnice start point
- Hike to Hala Gąsienicowa Murowaniec (stay 1 night)
- Hike to Dolina Pięciu Stawów (stay 1 night)
- Hike to Morskie Oko, take a horse carriage to the bus stop and then take a bus/shuttle back to Zakopane
<br/>
**Route Considerations:**
- Highly recommend the horse carriage down from Morskie Oko. We made this mistake of not doing this and it is a long walk on the road and not very scenic. You will be tired.
- Don’t recommend doing the listed route backwards, it is less scenic and more difficult in that direction
- Dolina Pięciu Stawów was our favorite Schronisko, but it is very difficult to get a spot there. We have included some alternate routes below if you cannot get a booking.
- You can climb Kozi Wierch or Rysy (highest mountain in Poland) via ferrata but make sure you do your research beforehand.
<br/>
**Website for Route Planning:** https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/#49.21526/20.07290/15
- Beware we are usually much faster than the estimated trail time but we found the time and distance estimates on this site optimistic. Technically you can do this route in <two nights but the sun sets early in the valley so I would not recommend this.
    - The route we took: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1etzi
        - We should have ended at the carriage stop at Włosienica: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1etzv
    - Most efficient route: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1etrz
    - Most scenic route: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1etz4
    - If you are not able to get a booking at Dolina Pięciu Stawów:
        - Second night at Dolina Rostoka: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1sjb5
            - Skips Morskie Oko but passes by an alpine waterfall instead.
            - Long second day so start early.
        - One Night at Murowaniec only: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1sjbo
            - Skips the valley but includes day trip to alpine pond and valley overlook.
        - Day Trip from Morskie Oko: https://mapa-turystyczna.pl/route/1sjbj
            - Steep climb from Morskie Oko to valley overlook, very beautiful though less dramatic than overlook from the other side.
<br/>
**Booking the “Schroniska” (shelters):**
- Murowaniec
    - Bookings should already be open
- Dolina Pięciu Stawów
    - Bookings should already be open. $45-$140 CAD per person.
    - After booking, you may need to manually complete a bank transfer and then send the confirmation to “rezerwacje@piecstawow.pl” within 24 hours. Confirm when booking.
- Blankets, sheets and a pillow can be added to your reservation.
<br/>
**FAQ**
- English is usually spoken at the shelters but most text is in Polish. Download offline Polish to English Google Translate.
- Most shelters take card but we recommend bringing cash just in case.
- Sometimes the only electricity plugs are at the front desk so bring a spare battery for your phone.
- Towels are not always available, bring a pack towel.
- Due to the remote nature of the huts they are occasionally not able to restock on supplies and bedding. Try to check-in early and bring cozy clothes in case of a shortage.
