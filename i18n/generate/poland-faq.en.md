###### **Do people speak English?**
Most young people and people in cities speak English. That being said, if you know a word or two in Polish people will either be very excited or very confused.

###### **Is it safe?**
Poland has one of the lowest crime rates in the EU, but as per usual it’s good to be vigilant. The war in Ukraine is not expected to directly affect Poland.

###### **How do I pay for things?**
While Poland is in the EU they have not adopted the Euro, and instead still use Polish Złoty (pronounced “zwoteh” and translates to “gold”). Złotys are typically 30-40% of CAD. Cards are accepted almost everywhere but it’s good to carry a little cash (Gotówka or “Goh-too-vka”) for: Older public bathrooms, small shops and stalls, tips. Tips are not required but 10% is appreciated in restaurants and you will usually not have the option to tip by card.

###### **How do I get around?**
_Within cities_: Walk or take public transit. Uber is also available.
_Between cities_: Poland has an extensive train network including both modern high speed lines and older soviet era locomotives. Some rural areas may be easier to reach by rental car. Recommend booking train tickets in advance.

###### **Do I need a Visa?**
Citizens of most western countries do not require a Visa to enter Poland. A list of countries requiring visa’s can be found here. Poland is part of the Schengen region.
