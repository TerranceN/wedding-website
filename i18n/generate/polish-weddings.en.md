Polish weddings follow a similar format to western weddings but with a few notable differences!
<br />
<br />
1) They go late! Be prepared to be dancing until dawn, or feel free to tap-out early (we might!)
2) There are multiple sit-down dinners to keep everyone going throughout the night. Save room!
3) The newlyweds are welcomed with bread, salt and vodka the later of which will be flowing freely.
4) Midnight is marked with “Oczepiny” celebrations.
5) There is an after party called “Poprawiny” the next day.
<br/>
<br/>
The wedding is in the Highlander (Góral) region of Poland where Paula’s Mother’s family comes from. We will be incorporating some local traditions from the region as well.
